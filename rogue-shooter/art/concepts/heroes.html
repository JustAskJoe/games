Rogue Shooter v2 · Heroes · Walk, attack and ability

The five heroes in motion

Every hero walks and attacks facing front, side and back; the game flips the side view for left. Walks are 4 frames. Base attacks and spacebar abilities are 3 frames each, with that class's effect. Shown at 6× for walks and 3× for attacks and abilities; in the game everything draws at 3×.

PLAYBACK

Knight

Attack: Sword Slash

Great helm, plume, plate, tabard, cape, kite shield. Attack: the longsword winds up over the shoulder and sweeps a wide golden arc in front.

Walk

Attack

Ability (Space): Shield Bash

Braces behind the shield, charges forward with speed lines and dust, and slams into the target with an impact burst and circling stun stars.

Mage

Attack: Magic Bolt

Old wizard with a bent hat, silver beard and starry robe. Attack: the orb charges with sparks, flares, and fires a glowing blue-violet homing bolt.

Walk

Attack

Ability (Space): Blink

A glyph ring forms at his feet, he dissolves in an arcane explosion, and reappears further ahead in a puff of sparkles.

Marksman

Attack: Piercing Shot

Blue hooded mantle, leather jerkin, quiver. Attack: raises the crossbow to aim, a muzzle flash, then a bolt flies out with a long piercing streak.

Walk

Attack

Ability (Space): Focus Shot

Locks a red reticle on the target while the crossbow charges, then fires a huge frost-blue piercing beam across the screen.

Rogue

Attack: Dagger Flurry

Peaked hood, mask, red scarf, twin daggers. Attack: two quick stabs, one per hand, each leaving a green slash, finished with a crossed X-cut.

Walk

Attack

Ability (Space): Shadow Dash

Crouches in a swirl of shadow smoke, then dashes forward leaving dark afterimages and a slash along the path.

Witch

Attack: Hex Pulse

Wide bent hat, ginger hair, ruffled dress, and now a crooked staff with a glowing magenta crystal. Attack: raises the staff, slams it down, and a hex ring pulses out around her.

Walk

Attack

Ability (Space): Ritual Burst

A rune circle opens under her, a pillar of light bursts up with a double hex ring, and hex marks appear on everything it touches.
