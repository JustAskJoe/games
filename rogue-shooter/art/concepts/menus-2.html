HP hits zero: the knight's death frames play and the screen tints red. What beat you is shown with its portrait, with a summary of the run. Try again keeps the hero; New hero goes to class select. Nothing carries over between runs because meta-progression is still undecided.

Planting with hover preview

Planting at round 12, when rare 1x2 crops are open. The camera zooms to the field, drawn at game size (16 px plots at 3x). The seed follows the cursor as a see-through plant over its footprint: green when it fits, red when it would cover a crop, a rock or the field edge. R rotates rare crops. The panel shows the seed bag (12 slots) and the picked seed.
