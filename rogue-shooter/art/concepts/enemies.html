Rogue Shooter v2 · Bestiary · 23 sprites

Every enemy, zone by zone

Redrawn in the hero style: material shading lit from the top-left, colored outlines and hand-placed details. Walkers face front, side and back (flip the side for left); fliers, blobs and fixed objects use one view. Each sprite sits on its zone's ground color, with the game's soft shadow. Shown at 4×; the game draws at 3×.
