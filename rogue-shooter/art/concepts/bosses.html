Rogue Shooter v2 · Bosses · 12 mini-bosses, 6 major bosses

Every boss, zone by zone

Every mini-boss and major boss in the hero style, facing the camera, with an idle loop and its signature attack. The two chargers (Pack Leader Wolf, Cavern Wyrm) also have a side view, and the Meadow Guardian a back view showing its weak spot. They are drawn full-size on the same pixel grid as the heroes (mini-bosses about 2× a normal enemy, major bosses about 3× a hero), so nothing is blown up. Effects and projectiles are separate strips. Shown at 3×, the size they appear in the game.
