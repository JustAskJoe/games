Rogue Shooter v2 · Farm · Building menus

Six buildings, six menus

What opens when you walk into each building. Every menu uses the shared kit (wood-trimmed panels, Press Start 2P at 8/16/24/32 px, 3 px borders, icons only at 2×/3×/4×), dressed in that building's own materials, and opens over the building's interior drawn at true game size. All six show the same mid-run moment: round 8, every building at tier 2, the same gold and materials. The last three screens show the Forge, Alchemy Lab and Arena later on, at tier 3, with their locked parts opened.

Forge · weapons

Iron plates with rivets and ember-orange type. Left: the five weapons, with level pips (orange = level, grey = up to the T2 cap of 5, faded = T3 levels), which two are equipped, and what an unowned weapon costs (25 gold + 2 of its signature material). Right: the selected weapon on the anvil, its next level and cost, the six mods (one per weapon, T2) and both evolutions with their stat gates (T3, level 5).

Alchemy Lab · potions

A parchment recipe book on a purple workbench. Tabs split the T1 potions, T2 elixirs, locked T3 tonics and conversion. Recipes you can't make yet are faded. The right page shows the selected recipe with each crop and material (have / need) and the Brew button; the side panel holds the Q/E/R belt and the 10-slot Potion Chest.

Luck gets a side panel showing how it shifts level-up card odds, now and after training. This is also the popup that appears after each round.
