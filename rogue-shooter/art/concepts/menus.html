Menus and the combat HUD

Five screens in the farm-hub style: title, class select, battle map, the combat HUD and the level-up pick. Each frame is a real 1280×800 game window: the art is 3× pixels, the menus are page elements like the game's current HUD (wood panels, square corners, Press Start 2P at multiples of 8 px, one 3 px border = one art pixel), so they can move into the build almost as-is. Every icon is a new 16×16 pixel icon, always at a whole-number size: 2× on small counters, 3× in lists, 4× as card art and in the ability slot.

3 · Battle map

Shown before each fight. Only the current zone's five rounds are on the map: cleared rounds are green with a check, the next round glows gold with your hero standing on it, and the boss round shows the boss as a silhouette. The side panel previews the enemies, the possible mini-bosses, the danger level and any Arena mutator, with Fight and Return to Farm.

4 · Combat HUD

Kept to the corners so the middle of the fight stays clear. Top left: portrait, level, HP and XP. Top middle: round, zone, timer and the current phase, with the mini-boss bar under it when one is out. Top right: gold, kills and the materials picked up this round. Bottom left: weapon slots with level pips (the second slot unlocks after the round 5 boss). Bottom middle: the spacebar ability with a stepped cooldown and the Q/E/R potion belt (R unlocks at Alchemy T2). Damage numbers are pixel font; crits are bigger and gold.

5 · Level-up pick

The fight pauses and dims. Three cards from the 12-card pool; the frame color is the rarity (wood common, blue rare, purple epic, gold legendary), and legendary cards get a slow shine. Each card shows its icon, the bonus, what it does in plain words and your running total. Cards your class prefers are tagged. Number keys pick directly; TAB still opens stats.
