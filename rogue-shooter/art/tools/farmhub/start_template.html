The farm on day one

What you see when a new run starts: only the cabin stands. Each building site is an empty lot or an old ruin, roped off with stakes and a blank sign with a hammer on it (no words on the art). Four crop plots are tilled and ready; the other 26 are buried under rocks, stumps, twigs and weeds until you clear them. The animal pen has a broken fence and no animals. Switch to "Fully built" to compare with the finished farm.

CABIN

Already yours. Same as the built farm: woodpile, bench, flower beds, picket fence.

FORGE LOT

A burned-out stone shell: broken walls, a cold furnace mouth, the chimney snapped off, rubble and a rusty anvil on its side.

ALCHEMY LAB LOT

A snapped tower stump with ivy, a bare timber frame, a cracked cauldron on its side and a few bottle shards.

SHRINE RUINS

The stone platform survives but is cracked and mossy. One pillar stands, one has fallen, the orb is gone and the braziers are tipped over.

OLD BARN

Collapsed: one leaning gable frame over a heap of red planks and old hay.

ARENA LOT

An empty dirt clearing ringed by half-buried stones, with weeds and a fallen banner pole.

TRAINING GROUNDS LOT

The sand yard is mostly grown over, with broken fence posts and a training dummy lying in the grass.

CROP FIELD

Plots 1, 2, 7 and 8 (top left) start tilled, with a hoe and watering can. The other 26 are covered, ready to be cleared as you unlock them.

ANIMAL PEN

Empty. The fence has gaps and a fallen rail, the trough is tipped over, and weeds grow through the mud.
