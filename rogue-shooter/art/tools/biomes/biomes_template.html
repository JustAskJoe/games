Where the fights happen

Each zone is built from game-ready pieces: a 96×96 ground tile that repeats seamlessly, obstacles you bump into, and small ground details you walk over. The screen at the top of each zone is assembled from exactly those pieces plus the new hero and enemy sprites, then lit for the zone. It shows one 1280×800 window at the game's 3× pixel size.
