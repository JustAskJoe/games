Team pitch from Hunter, answered in Forge at Deep from the Moonwort & Co. Crew Pitch (Oct 27, 2026). Draven and the rest of the crew haven't answered yet, so treat every decision below as a draft until the crew talks it through.

## The hook
A cozy apothecary sim. You run the little shop at the edge of Little Hollow: neighbors knock with a trouble ("I can't sleep"), you grow and forage herbs by the real moon and season, and you brew something to help. Every leaf opens a true lore card. On your phone, or hung on your living-room wall in mixed reality.

## Signature mechanic: needs, not orders
Villagers describe a trouble, never a recipe. Ingredients carry 2 to 3 traits (warming / cooling, calming / brightening, drying / soothing), and how you process them (steep, grind, dry, infuse, distill, leave under the moon) changes those traits. A brew is a recipe of traits, so many paths work. Picking the brew is reading people, not filling a quota. Wrong combos make funny named duds (a "Tonic of Mild Regret" that makes a villager hiccup in Latin), never a fail.

## Decisions
- **Player:** three hypotheses to test in Prototype 1: the Cozy Unwinder (adult, Stardew / Potion Craft, buys premium, hates ads), the Curious Kid (8 to 14, often with a parent), and the Hedge Witch (herbalism and folklore fans who want accuracy).
- **Platform:** the phone is the business (portrait, one thumb). Pico mixed reality is an optional bonus room; Quest later through OpenXR. Prototype 1 is a phone-first web build.
- **Modes:** solo, with a daily ritual card. No multiplayer, trading or shop visits in v1.
- **Controls:** tactile gestures: circle to grind, drag to pour, tilt to stir, tap to cork, with haptics. MR is hands first, controllers as fallback, no locomotion.
- **Sessions:** five-minute sips; a full in-game day in 5 to 15 minutes; MR sessions 10 to 20.
- **Loop:** dawn card and the real moon → garden and forage → shop hours (read a trouble, pick a brew) → workbench (grind, steep, stir, cork; new combos go in the grimoire) → dusk (decorate, read, sleep). The shop is the spine.
- **Economy:** coin from helping villagers buys seeds and jars. Trust is the deeper currency: it unlocks villager stories, forage spots and rarer ingredients. No energy meters.
- **Failure:** none by design. Duds are still sellable, missing a day costs nothing, streaks reward but never punish.
- **Progression:** grimoire completion, a shop that visibly fills up, a garden from 3 to 12 beds, villager arcs. Launch targets: 40 ingredients, about 60 brews, 12 villagers, about 40 ritual cards, 5 forage spots, 4 seasons with a festival each.
- **World:** folk craft and plant wisdom with real folklore and plant history underneath. Real seasons and moon phase change what grows and brews best. Short, warm writing; a familiar (cat, toad or crow) that drops hints.
- **Art:** painterly 3D, candle-lit and ornate: warm, curious, tactile. Plum night, moss, beeswax gold, rose clay, ember. Tarot-style ingredient cards and Art Nouveau gold frames; a sister look to Trickbound. The deck art is AI mood reference, not final.
- **Business:** premium. Mobile $5.99 to $7.99 one time; Pico MR $14.99 to $19.99 or a cross-buy unlock; seasonal expansions $2.99 to $4.99 (all estimates). No ads, energy or gacha, ever.
- **Rating:** everyone, with the 18+ "Apothecary" door still an open call (see below).

## Prototype 1
One in-game day: the morning card, 1 garden bed, 1 forage spot, 10 ingredients, 12 brews including duds, 3 villagers, and a grimoire with real lore cards. Greybox 3D is fine. Phone-first web build, about 2 to 3 weeks, plus a 1-week Pico MR spike (the cupboard on a real wall, the mortar on a real table).

**The one question it answers:** is reading a villager's trouble and discovering the right brew fun on its own?
**Pass bar:** at least 4 of 5 testers find a brew without help, ask to keep playing, and recall one real plant fact the next day. First brew within 90 seconds of install.

## Risks
- Scope: four loops is a lot. The shop is the spine; garden, forage and decor stay thin until brewing is fun.
- Apothecary liability and store rating: tinctures (alcohol) could raise the rating for the whole app. Never medical advice, never "cures"; needs expert and legal review.
- Respect: leave closed or sacred practices out, credit sources, no wild-foraging instructions.
- "Witchcraft for kids" backlash: frame it as folklore and plant history.
- 3D art cost for a tiny team: a stylized reusable kit, and MR stays optional.

## Open for the crew

- Tincture option: A in-app 18+, B kitchen-safe in-app with tinctures on the web (current lean), or C lore-only v1 with the Apothecary later.
- Engine for the full game: Unity (OpenXR + Pico SDK) is proposed, not confirmed.
- Who reviews the Apothecary content.
- Not covered yet: the first-session lessons, how brew quality and villager moods read at a glance, audio direction, hours per week, and the plan after Prototype 1.
