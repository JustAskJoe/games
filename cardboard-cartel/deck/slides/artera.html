Every era paints differently

Founders

Expressionist oil

Thick palette-knife strokes, muted plum and charcoal, one burning accent. Vintage cards feel like old paintings.

V Reader

Painterly, moody

The house style. Rich painted scenes, atmospheric light, glowing accents in the dark. Most of the game looks like this.

Emerald

Clean and cinematic

Crisp, bright modern painting with rim light. The full-art chase of today.

The paint style is itself a collecting signal.
