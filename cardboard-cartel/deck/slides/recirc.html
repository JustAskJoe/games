Recirculation, not reprints

Supply comes back

Released cards sit with rivals and NPCs. When a rival retires, their whole collection hits the market: an estate find made of existing supply.

Supply goes away

The Bulk Shredder, Master Binder set-locks and damage remove cards for good.

Result: out-of-print sets genuinely get rarer over time.
