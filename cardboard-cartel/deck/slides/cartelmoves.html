The Cartel’s weekly moves

Flood

Dumps a subset: price drops ~30% for 48 hours, then recovers to ~80% over the week.

Buy-back bounty

Pays up to 140% for specific cards it’s missing.

Pallet drop

A mixed lot sold by blind auction against rivals.

Heat

Beat them too often and they lock you out of direct drops for a few days. Estates stay open.
