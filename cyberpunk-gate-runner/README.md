# Cyberpunk Gate Runner (working title: Overclock)

© 2026 justaskjoe. All rights reserved.

A cyberpunk runner shooter for phones. Steer a growing squad through neon streets, pick gates that multiply your shooters, and blast through enemies, all synced to a chill house beat.

**Status:** Pitch approved in Forge, Oct 31, 2026 (solo pitch from Zach; crew review pending). **Next:** Prototype 1.

**Read first:** [`pitch/brief.md`](pitch/brief.md). What changed lately, and what's waiting: [`patch-notes.md`](patch-notes.md).

## Prototype 1 (planned)
- 1 street of about 60 seconds, steered with one thumb; the squad fires on its own.
- 3 gate types (add shooters, multiply shooters, fire rate), 2 enemy types, 1 weapon pickup, 1 mini boss.
- 1 placeholder house loop with gates pulsing on the beat, reusing the Neon Surf beat engine.
- **Pass bar:** everyone plays a second run without being asked.

No build yet. Once there is one, it goes in `builds/` and gets an entry in [`../games.json`](../games.json) so it shows up in the Lab.
