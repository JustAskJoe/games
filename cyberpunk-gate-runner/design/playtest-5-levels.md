# Gate Runner: five levels playtest

© 2026 justaskjoe. All rights reserved.

For Zach and the kids, in the Lab preview. Play on a phone if you can, with sound on, and add `#fps` to the URL once to note the frame rate. Pause (≡) → **Jump to boss** gets you straight to a fight. Those runs don't save.

## Every level
- [ ] You can tell which level you're on at a glance, from the colors alone.
- [ ] The music feels new but still part of the same soundtrack.
- [ ] You worked out what the boss wants without anyone telling you. Note how many tries it took.
- [ ] The loss message told you something useful.
- [ ] It felt one clear step harder than the level before.

## Level 1: Night Market, Hush Enforcer
- [ ] **Does it still feel as hard but doable as the build you played?** This is the main check. It's retuned, and only a kill counts now.
- [ ] Your squad stays smaller than before (about 50, not 140). Does growing it still feel good?
- [ ] The glowing half of the road warns you in time to step off it.
- [ ] Track: the same one you know.

## Level 2: Signal Yards, Mute Carrier
- [ ] You notice the core takes no damage ("ARMOR" on the boss bar) and work out why.
- [ ] You can line up with a pod at each end of the barge. "SHOOT" labels are readable.
- [ ] "ARMOR DOWN!" feels like a reward, and the window is long enough to do real damage.
- [ ] Teal and amber, low skyline. Track: 124 BPM, C minor, bouncy pluck lead.
- [ ] This is the level bots got stuck on most. Does it feel like a wall?

## Level 3: Glass Canyon, Mirror Warden
- [ ] You can tell which side the mirror is on, and that it flashes before it moves.
- [ ] "REFLECTED" makes clear you caused the damage.
- [ ] Moving with the mirror is hard but fair, especially when it speeds up below 40% health.
- [ ] Icy blue, tall towers. Track: 120 BPM, E minor, glassy bell lead, lots of space.

## Level 4: Static District, Echo
- [ ] You can spot the real Echo. It's pink and pulses on the kick; the fakes are violet.
- [ ] It still works with the sound off.
- [ ] Shockwaves from the fakes' lanes make sense, rather than feeling random.
- [ ] Violet and acid green, heavy fog. Does the fog ever hide something you needed?
- [ ] Track: 118 BPM, D minor, dreamy half-time feel. Does it hold up against the action?

## Level 5: The Conservatory, Director Vale
- [ ] Running into a gold orb feels right, after four levels of dodging.
- [ ] Gold and red are easy to tell apart at speed, including for anyone colorblind. The CATCH and DODGE labels should help.
- [ ] You notice your squad stops firing inside the silence beam.
- [ ] Feels like one more step, not an ending. The clear screen should say more streets are coming.
- [ ] Crimson and gold. Track: 124 BPM, F# minor, bright saw lead, full groove.

## Progress
- [ ] Beat a boss, refresh the page: the next level is still open, with your squad and gun.
- [ ] Level select shows what each level starts you with.
- [ ] Locked levels can't be started.
- [ ] Reset progress asks first, then locks everything.
- [ ] Your gun carries over, and the fork offers keep or switch.
- [ ] A weak clear of a level never leaves you stuck.
