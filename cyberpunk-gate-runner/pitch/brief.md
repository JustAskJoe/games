# Cyberpunk Gate Runner (working title: Overclock) · Brief

Solo pitch from Zach, answered in Pitch Forge and approved there on October 31, 2026. Hunter and Joe haven't answered or discussed it yet, so treat every decision below as a draft until the crew talks it through.

## The hook
The runner shooter the mobile ads keep promising, actually delivered. Steer a growing squad through neon streets, pick gates that multiply your shooters, and blast through enemies, all synced to a chill house beat.

## Signature mechanic: The Drop
Every street follows a real track structure: intro, build, drop. Gates hit on the beat during the build charge Overclock. At the drop, Overclock fires: weapons evolve, the city lights up, and the boss or big horde hits at the same moment.

## Decisions
- **Player:** casual mobile, adults 18 to 35 who saw the ads and wanted the real game. Streamers secondary.
- **Platform:** prototype in the phone first web kit; launch on iOS and Android. Steam PC is a maybe.
- **Modes:** solo campaign and endless at launch; daily challenge as a stretch. No PvP or co-op.
- **Controls:** one thumb steering; the squad fires on its own. Beat timing is a bonus, never a requirement.
- **Runs:** 60 to 90 seconds; sessions of 5 to 15 minutes. Squad count is health; retry is one tap.
- **Loop:** gear up, run, drop, cash out.
- **Progression:** 5 districts with 5 streets each, a boss per district. Weapons and shooter classes unlock with chips; credits feed a permanent upgrade tree.
- **World:** Neon City, 2089. The megacorp Hush has banned music; you free it district by district, and each freed district lights up and unlocks its track.
- **Art:** neon synthwave; glowing, readable, chill. Deep navy with hot pink, cyan and violet. Cyan squad, pink enemies, color coded gates.
- **Audio:** original deep house and melodic techno, 118 to 124 BPM, one track per district. SFX tuned to key and tempo.
- **Business:** free to play with opt in rewarded ads, a no ads purchase and cosmetics. Never pay to win, never forced ads, no energy timers.

## Prototype 1
1 street of about 60 seconds, 3 gate types, 2 enemy types, 1 weapon pickup, 1 mini boss, 1 house loop with beat synced gates. Built in the kit, reusing the Neon Surf beat engine.

**The one question it answers:** does steering a growing squad through gates on the beat feel good enough to replay?
**Pass bar:** everyone plays a second run without being asked.

## Risks
- Beat sync could feel forced. Keep timing a bonus.
- Phone browser audio latency. Lean on the Neon Surf calibration.
- A crowded, cheap to copy genre, and free to play needs ad spend we don't have.
- Original music and art for 5 districts is the biggest content cost.
