- Asset: the Arrow tower, base plus its two tier II upgrade branches (A: Pierce, B: Multishot), and its tower button.
- Where it shows (screen, size on a phone): on the board during waves, one 80 px cell wide and up to 96 px tall, drawn 8 px above its cell (the 960 × 640 board is scaled down to fit, so on a 390 px-wide phone in landscape a cell is roughly 55–70 px). Also the 96 × 96 tower button.
- States / upgrades / animations:
  - Base (tier I)
  - Pierce II (branch A, rust mark): "Arrows pass through, hitting up to 3 enemies in a line."
  - Multishot II (branch B, gold mark): "Fires twice as fast."
  - No animation asked for. The prototype bobs the tower 2 px when it fires; keep that.
- Must read as: "cheap, quick, reliable", the first tower you trust; Pierce looks heavy and focused, Multishot looks spread and busy.
- Directions:
  - A: **Painted.** `art-direction.md` as written: ink linework over painterly flat color. Timber plank body on a stone footing, cream plaster lookout band with arrow slits, tall teal slate cone roof, small pennant. Pierce goes taller and iron-banded, with one heavy rust-tipped ballista bolt aimed forward. Multishot gets a wider band, two little side turrets and a fan of five gold-tipped arrows behind the roof. Sheet: `art/concepts/9-A.svg`.
  - B: **Pixel.** The same tower, the same shapes, as 40 × 48 pixel art shown at exactly 2x with nearest-neighbor scaling, palette-locked to `art-direction.md`, with a 1 px ink outline. Sheet: `art/concepts/9-B.svg`.
  - C: none (the request asks for exactly two).
- Upgrade rule (from `art-direction.md`): same silhouette family, bigger and more ornate; rust mark for A, gold mark for B, plus the prototype's "II" ribbon.
