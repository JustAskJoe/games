## What to watch for
- **The draft:** do people read all three cards, or always grab the same kind? The smart bot always takes Spyglass and Drill Sergeant first, and it wins about 55%. Do humans land on the same picks, or find something better?
- **The road growing (waves 4 and 7):** does anyone get caught out with towers in the wrong place? Is that a fun "rebuild" moment or a punishment?
- **Watching:** the brief says players watch during waves. If it reads as boring, note when (early waves at 1×? late waves at 3×?). This matters for co-op, where idle time doubles.
- **Counters:** without Pell (the coach) on, do people still figure out Cannons for Brutes and Spark for Flyers from the wave preview?
- **Music:** on or off by the end? Any comment on it at all?
- **Late game:** use Tester tools → Jump to wave 7 or 10 so every session sees the Warlord.
