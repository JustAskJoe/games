# Tower Defense (working title) · Brief

Approved direction from the crew session on October 19, 2026. Hunter and Joe answered the Pitch Forge questionnaire separately; these are the decisions that came out of the discussion guide.

## The hook
Co-op roguelite tower defense. A deck inside each run; a keep the crew builds between runs. Prototype the run first; the keep comes in only if the run is fun.

## Decisions
- **Modes:** solo + co-op at launch. 3v1 (one player controls the waves) scoped as a stretch. No PvP.
- **Platform:** prototype in the phone-first web kit; launch on Steam PC.
- **Deck:** a true deckbuilder: buffs, passives and things you build toward. Some cards affect towers or the keep. The keep (management) decides which towers you can field.
- **Towers:** 20+ themed towers with branching upgrades. Merging is in scope review.
- **During waves:** players watch.
- **Runs:** about 20 minutes, 10 to 12 waves. Shared health pool; teammates can buy a downed player back in; pool at zero ends the run.
- **Art:** ink linework over painterly flat color (teal slate, warm timber, cream plaster, moss, dusty-rose sky). Music follows the look.
- **Business:** premium on Steam with a free demo.
- **Market:** co-op TDs exist (Dungeon Defenders, Bloons TD 6 co-op). Our gap is co-op roguelite TD with deckbuilding; verify before any external slide. Roguelite TD is crowded, so co-op must be the standout.
- **Time:** Hunter and Joe about 10 hours a week each.

## Prototype 1 (built)
Solo. 1 map whose path expands twice, 4 towers (Arrow, Cannon, Spark, Frost) with two upgrade branches each, 5 enemy types (Grunt, Runner, Flyer, Brute, Warlord), 10 waves, a card draft (3 offered, pick 1) after each wave, keep health, leaks cost health.

**The one question it answers:** is drafting a card after each wave, on a map that keeps growing, fun enough to replay?
**Pass bar:** both of you choose to play a second run without being asked.
