Two real growth patterns from the founder's own setup. First, a single job climbs tiers: the grocery order started as a reminder-style sheet, gained a message, then read live prices, and finally became an errand that fills the cart without ever placing the order. Second, related automations cluster into a district: eight money watchers share one tracker sheet, so in the game the bank grows into Money Row, with the observatory tallying the monthly total. This is exactly the expansion loop the game rewards.
