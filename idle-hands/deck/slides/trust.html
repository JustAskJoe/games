Safety as a mechanic

Buildings earn autopilot. They don't start with it.

TRUST 1

Ask every time

New buildings draft the action and wait for your tap.

TRUST 2

Notify after

After a run of approved actions, it acts and tells you.

TRUST 3

Autopilot

Fully idle, within limits you set: spend caps, recipients, hours.

Money, new recipients and anything irreversible always ask, at every trust level. The answer to "what if it buys 40 pounds of bananas" is built into the fun.
