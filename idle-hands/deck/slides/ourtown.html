This is the founder's actual setup, built over a weekend of conversations with Claude. Every card is a real, scheduled automation running today: the studio's daily numbers, a morning email for his wife with a newly generated picture each day, his own morning brief, the CFO's 7 am numbers, a sign-up form that checks people's roles in the studio software, a three-step weekly grocery order, eight personal-money watchers (subscriptions, price hikes, bills, lease renewal, refunds and price drops, forgotten credits, class-action settlements, unclaimed property), a monthly money report, an inbox digest, and weekly sweepstakes and paid-study scouts. Idle Hands makes this journey possible for people who will never write a prompt.
