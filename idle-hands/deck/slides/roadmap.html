Timeline assumes a core team of four with heavy AI-assisted development: the founder, a technical co-founder and AI agents that build the engine, the client, content and tests under human review. Nothing moves to the next milestone until its gate is met. The engine is separate from the town, so Steam and XR reuse the same backend, account system and 3D models; only the client changes. The founder's own town (19 automations already running) is the proof that the engine side works before a line of game code.
