Design rule for every new automation type: put it in the building where that job would happen in a real town. Players learn the map intuitively, and the town plans itself into districts: home row (lanterns, garden, cottages), the lane (post office, library, bakery), market street (store, bank, market hall), the works (workshop), and the harbor and rail edge (station, lighthouse).
