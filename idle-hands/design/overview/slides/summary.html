At a glance

A cozy town that makes your life run itself.

What it is

An idle game where each building runs a real automation, from reminders up to errands and AI agents.

Who it’s for

Anyone who wants AI help with life admin but would never write a prompt.

How it earns

Free town, Pro subscription with AI included, Energy top-ups and cosmetic skins. No ads.

Where it runs

Web and phone first, then Steam, then XR as a tabletop town.

How it’s built

A small team with AI agents writing most of the code, on a managed integration platform.

Where it stands

Foundations phase. 19 real automations already run, and the engine plan is written.

Status as of October 2026: the project is in its foundations phase. The founder’s own 19 automations prove the engine side works; the next steps are the engine API spec, a clickable town prototype with the first four buildings, and an art pipeline test. Errands are the top tier of automation: actions in the outside world such as placing a grocery order, paying a bill or booking an appointment, each with a confirm step.
