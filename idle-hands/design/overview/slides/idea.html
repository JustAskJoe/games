The gap isn’t capability, it’s imagination and trust. Idle games are about building systems that run while you’re away, and so is life automation. Every comp either gamifies effort (focus timers, habit pets) or automates life (assistants). Nobody has made the automation itself the game. A game’s progression ladder suggests the next automation and builds trust one small win at a time.
