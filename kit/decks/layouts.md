# Pitch deck slide layouts and rules

Every slide is a `<section>` on a 1920×1080 canvas with inline styles. The section style is always:
`background:…;color:…;font-family:<body>;padding:128px 128px 160px;display:flex;flex-direction:column;gap:40px`
Helper names refer to `gen_deck.py`.

## Deck rules

1. **Text is at least 24px everywhere.** That includes footers, captions, table cells and pills. Body text is 26–30px, card titles 32px, h2 56–72px (64 on art slides, 96 for statements), h1 136px or more.
2. **Footer:** `position:absolute;left:128px;bottom:64px;width:1664px;font-size:24px;color:<muted>`, written as `ONE FIFTY LABS · GAME · NN · <extra>`. Leave it off the cover and the ask. Content padding is `128px 128px 160px` so nothing runs into the footer. Art slides with tighter padding (`88/96px 128px 140px`) can drop the footer to `bottom:56px`.
3. **Ownership notice on every slide:** `<p style="position:absolute;right:128px;top:44px;width:1100px;text-align:right;font-size:24px;color:<muted>">© 2026 F Lane d/b/a One Fifty Labs · All rights reserved</p>`.
4. **Label AI art.** Put "AI mood reference, not final art" in the footer extra or the image caption. Mockups say "Layout mockup, not final art". Mocks built from AI art say "Mock card built from AI mood art". Write real `alt` text for every image.
5. **Sources go in the speaker notes** (`<aside>`): who reported it, which outlet, and the date. The slide only carries a short footer tag such as "Sources in speaker notes", "Sources in notes" or "Prices are proposals to test". The notes should also flag estimates and placeholder names.
6. **Owner requirement:** at least 7 art slides in a full deck. Mix full-bleed art, a grid, anatomy, a palette, a world view and a screen mockup.
7. **Fonts** go in `deck.json` `faces` with Google Fonts hrefs. Use one display face and one body face (Fraunces + Nunito Sans for the Tilebound family, Oswald + Rubik for Cardboard Cartel, Fredoka + Nunito Sans for Idle Hands). Define the palette once as theme constants.
8. **Images:** upload images to the artifact as assets **first** (`asset: true`, up to 25 per call), record `{key: id}` in `blobs.json`, then reference them as `/_blob/<id>`. The generator prints any `PENDING_` keys that still need uploading.
9. **Publish order:** `deck.json` + `cover` first, then the slides in batches (by section). Run `render_check.py` locally before publishing. It catches overflow, text under 24px, a missing notice or notes, and `<x-shape>` elements. A gray 300px ellipse dead center is the editor's default shape, added by accident (one turned up on the Tilebound Short Pitch proof slide). Ask F before removing a shape you didn't put there. The Slides type says not to re-verify a deck after it's published unless F asks.
10. **Sections:** each section in `deck.json` gets a one-sentence `description` and a `start` slide id. Typical decks have 4–6 sections. Big internal decks split sections (s8, s8b, s8c).
