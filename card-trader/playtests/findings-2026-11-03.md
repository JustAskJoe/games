## Top findings
1. **Shops are broke by day 6, so the show doesn't happen.** Cash after day 6's rent is under 100₵ in most runs (0₵ in 9 of 24), and the 40₵ table goes unbooked in 14 of 24 runs. The same problem shows up as "a safer way to cover rent mid-run" in 20 of 24 verdicts, the most common ask after the fake reminder.
   - **Options:**
     - (a) take the table fee out of the show's sales instead of up front;
     - (b) make the table cheaper (20₵);
     - (c) the long-standing mid-run cash floor (plan section 6: a small "regulars" income tied to stars, or a bulk buyback when cash is under rent).
   - **My pick:** (a) now, and (c) as the real fix.
2. **Consignment is a hit, but it's nearly free money.** 88% of Orrin's cards sell, and the space trade-off rarely bites. Same finding as the balance sim (4.8 of 5).
   - **Options:** keep his cards out of the show, raise his minimums, or have him take back 1–2 cards early if they sit in the back.
3. **The contract works and reads clearly.** About half the cards get found. The bots can't see the stretch card's true grade, so they only find it by trying. Humans should tell us whether "grade 8+" feels fair with only a range to go on.
4. **Still open from last round:**
   - a reminder to re-check stock for fakes (21 of 24);
   - fakes unclear for kid, cozy and trusting;
   - thieves unclear for kid and goblin;
   - the board never touched by 4 of 8 personas;
   - repacks never seen, as expected, since they're off in v3.1.
5. **The rating explains itself now.** "Rating unclear" fell to 2 personas (kid, goblin), both of whom skip every hint. The beta gate's "clear 3+ for 6 of 8" passes on these numbers.

## Not to trust
- **"Sleeves unfun" (6 of 8) and "add auto-sleeve / stock the floor" (6 of 24) are partly the bots' own habits.** Auto-sleeve and Stock the floor exist since v3.1, but these personas still sleeve and place cards by hand, and each refusal counts as a setback. Worth teaching them to use the buttons before trusting this.
- **The show's "remove" votes mean "never came up"**, mostly because the shop was broke (finding 1), not because anyone disliked it.
- **The personas' contract and consignment rules are new** (written for this round): readers and collectors take the contract, and vintage lovers and readers take Orrin's cards.
