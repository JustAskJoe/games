# Card Trader v3 playtest findings, Nov 1, 2026 (agents)

© 2026 justaskjoe. All rights reserved.

Two kinds of agent playtest, side by side. Human answers aren't in yet.
- **Tier A:** 8 scripted personas × 3 runs (seeds 1017, 1034, 1051), answers derived from telemetry. Details: `agents-2026-11-01.md`.
- **No-API Tier B:** Claude played 2 full runs itself through `prototype/play-driver.js`, seeing only player-visible text and deciding every stop as the persona. Runs and notes: `claude-2026-11-01.json`.
  - **Cautious newbie** (seed 2001, singles/balanced/shell): Lane Favorite, 542₵ → 1,614₵ (+198%), 3.7★.
  - **Impatient min-maxer** (seed 2002, singles/cash/star): Lane Favorite, 690₵ → 2,383₵ (+245%), 3.9★.

Tier A counts walls and money; Claude reads the words. Where both point the same way, it's a strong signal. Where only Claude sees it, it's usually text or a strategy a fixed bot never tries.

## Text and bugs (Claude only)

These were found by reading. Tier A can't see them.
- Day 8's close says "Wave 3 reprint list is out: ... is on it" on the eve of Wave 4 (the same line as day 4). Both runs.
- Card Night with zero packs on the rack: "The crowd still bought some packs".
- **Possible bug, not verified in the browser:** card names can come out `undefined` after a save is loaded. `CT.load()` doesn't rebuild the card universe, so `tmpl()` names are undefined until something calls `uni(G)`. Reproduced in Node through the driver (a buyer said "I'm looking for undefined"). `frontend.js` loads saves on continue, so check it there.

## What worked
- The day 1 checklist: the newbie set up with zero refusals. The min-maxer, which tried to open first, got one clear refusal and fixed it.
- DJ Vee's warnings land: the Scale Gang warning led to selling behind the counter, and the promo warning led to the Spectrometer.
- Watching catches thieves, kids and drifters reliably, and the kid's parent scene and the drifter's "buy my finds" read well.
- Ordering sealed and racking it is a clear, satisfying loop (a 60₵ blister sold for 135₵ off the rack on day 2).
