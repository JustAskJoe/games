## A 5-day week
Days 1 to 3 stay roughly as v1. Days 4 and 5 add the bad actors and one big market event, so the week builds to a climax:
1. **Reef Run:** learn the Loupe and haggling (as v1).
2. **Hive Swarm:** trade lanes matter, then Card Night (as v1).
3. **Star Drift:** grading pays off, and the first **scalper** shows up.
4. **The Rumor:** a market manipulator pumps one card ("Prime Queen Zero is about to spike!"). Do you buy in, sell into the hype, or call it out?
5. **Set Release Day:** a new set drops. Lines at the door, scalpers, pre-orders, a customs sweep, and Grade Day for the whole week.

## Bad actors (the "probably stolen" side, our own take)
| Who | What they do | How you counter | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scalper** | Buys every sealed pack and hot single at your price, then resells on the strip at 2× | Purchase limits, a "regulars first" list, raising prices on hot items | Pricing power vs. reputation |
| **Pump crew** | Spreads a rumor (Lane FM ticker, fake streamer hype), dumps their copies on you, and the price crashes the next day | Check the source (who's actually docking?), sell into the pump, or ignore it | Reading the market vs. trusting hype |
| **Fake ring** | Better fakes that pass UV. They only fail the Loupe's surface or edge check, or the grader | Upgrade the Loupe (a spectrometer filter), keep a "known forger" list | The Loupe as a progression path |
| **Swap scammer** | Asks to see a card, hands back a fake from their sleeve | Watch the hands: one quick tap on a "they're swapping!" moment, or slab your best cards | A short attention mini-moment |
| **Hot goods** | A relic card smuggled out of a system that bans selling it; the owner's faction is looking | Buy and risk customs, refuse, or report it for faction trust | Factions and reputation with each species |
| **Shill bidder** | At Card Night, bids up auction lots with a friend | Spot the pattern, cap your bids | Auctions (a new mechanic) |
| **Return fraud** | Buys a card, swaps in a damaged copy, and asks for a refund | A no-refunds sign (rep hit) or checking returns under the Loupe | Store policy as a choice |

## More mechanics to try (pick 3 or 4, not all)
- **Store policy board:** set purchase limits, a refund policy, and a "we grade on site" sign. Each changes who walks in.
- **Faction trust per species:** in place of one rep number. It decides who docks at your door (it's in the brief).
- **Auctions at Card Night:** you run a lot, the crowd bids, and shills exist.
- **Loupe upgrades:** spectrometer (better fakes), script lens (cheaper decoding), steady hands (tools cost less patience).
- **Rival stall across the strip:** undercuts your prices on hot cards and sometimes buys out a seller before you.
- **Customs sweep:** random checks punish holding hot goods.
- **Pre-orders for Set Release Day:** take deposits now, deliver on day 5.
