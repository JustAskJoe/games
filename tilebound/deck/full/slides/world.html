THE ISLAND STORY

Tessera Isle: a fresh start, one town at a time.

You arrive in Kettle Cove for a new chapter, join the little club at the Tile & Teacup, and learn that every town on the island has a parlor, a house card and a master.

Cinder Quay

Ember Noodle Bar on the docks. Fast, bold play. Master: Old Mags.

Tidewater

A teahouse on stilts over the bay. Patient readers. Master: Captain Ruu.

Fernhollow

A greenhouse garden club. Blossom hands. Master: Professor Ivy.

Gale Heights

A rooftop lounge, open late. Wind tricksters. Master: Juno.

FINALE

Beat all four masters to sit at the Drake Hall table, where the island’s oldest club keeps the Crimson Drake.
