PRODUCTION PIPELINE

Every change goes through the bots before it reaches people.

1 Design

Edit a tile, a card line or a rule in the data sheet

2 Simulate

Bots play 10,000 games; fail a target, back to 1

3 Make

Illustrations, outfits, tile sets, music and writing

4 Build

Every push tests, builds and posts a playtest link

5 Playtest

Real club players, watched, every two weeks

Content: sheets → JSON, checked at build time

Art: AI sketches, artists paint, sprite atlases

Writing: AI drafts, a person edits every line

Builds: GitHub Actions → web, TestFlight; PICO CLI for XR
