THE TABLE

Real American-style mahjong, four at a table.

1 · The Swap

Before each hand, everyone passes three tiles right, across, then left. Mimics can’t be passed.

2 · Draw and discard

Draw from the wall, discard into the pond. In master matches, your favored tiles sit in your side of the wall.

3 · Call and expose

Claim any discard to finish a set of three or more and lay it face up. Later, exposed sets wake critter abilities.

4 · Mahj!

Match a hand on the parlor’s House Card to win. The discarder pays double; a self-drawn win, everyone does.

One four-seat ruleset runs every table in the game, following American mahjong: a card of target hands, the Swap before play, jokers (Mimics) that fill sets but never pairs, calls for exposed sets, and payouts where the discarder pays double. Tutorial lessons use the same rules with fewer seats and tiles. Tilebound twists: favored tiles from your collection and, later, critter abilities. All hands on our cards are original; we never reproduce the National Mah Jongg League card. Full detail: “Tilebound: Prototype Rules Sheet”.
