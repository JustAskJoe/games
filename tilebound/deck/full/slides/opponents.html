OPPONENTS

Every opponent plays like a person you know.

The Gentle One

Rarely calls, sometimes slips. Route travelers and new club bots.

The Reader

Watches your discards and never feeds you. Teaches defense.

The Racer

Calls everything, chases the cheapest line. Fast hands.

The Master

Almost no mistakes, leans hard into the house style.

Under the hood, one piece of math, “how many tiles from Ready”, drives every opponent, the newcomer hints and the balance bots. Personality is a handful of numbers per character.

Every table seats four, so three of these sit with you each game: regulars at casual tables, a master and two regulars in a master match, club friends at Mahj Night.
