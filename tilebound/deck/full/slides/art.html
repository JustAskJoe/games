ART DIRECTION

Illustrated 2D: a vintage mahjong set come to life.

  • 1920s Art Nouveau linework and gold-leaf frames, like an heirloom set
  • Flat jewel-tone color on warm paper
  • A light touch of folk magic: moons, stars, herbs, candlelight
  • Round, cute critters, framed like tile carvings
  • Built in layers: one tile frame, swappable critters and finishes

In the spirit of Strange Horticulture, Cozy Grove and Spiritfarer: small teams whose hand-made 2D art became the reason people bought in.

Why illustrated 2D: the tiles are the hero, and mahjong already has a century of gorgeous tile design to draw on. A vintage Art Nouveau set with a little folk magic gives Tilebound a look no creature collector or mahjong app owns. 2D is also the cheapest art to make well: layered tiles, portrait paint-overs and painted town backdrops, with no modeling, rigging or lighting. It runs on any phone and stays crisp on iPad.
