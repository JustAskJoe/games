MAHJ NIGHT

A four-seat table, every week.

Once a week the club gathers for a full four-player game. Who sits with you depends on who you’ve grown close to, and everyone plays like themselves.

It’s the heart of the club, and it needs no internet and no other players.

Rotating guests · a master drops in now and then

House rules · pick the card, abilities on or off

Table talk · gossip and story between hands

Weekly prizes · a tile bag or an outfit piece

Show off · friends notice your look and tiles

Later, maybe · invite real friends online

Mahj Night delivers the social feeling of a club without servers: the same rules engine runs with four seats, filled by AI club friends with their own play styles. Online play with real friends is deliberately out of scope; the headless engine keeps it possible as a later update if the game takes off.
