Ember, Tide and Grove stand in for the three suits; Winds, Drakes, Blossoms and Mimics stand in for winds, dragons, flowers and jokers. As in American mahjong, Mimics can fill sets of three or more but never a pair or a single. All names are first-pass placeholders.
