SOUND

The click of the tiles is the star.

Real tile sounds

Recorded from real sets: the clack of a discard, the shuffle, the slide of a set laid down. Each finish sounds a little different.

Lo-fi music

Warm keys, soft drums and a touch of 1920s parlor jazz. One theme per town, a gentle table loop, a big master track.

Critter voices

Tiny chirps and squeaks when a critter wakes. No voice acting for people: text, warm sound effects, and room for your own music.

Many players will have the sound off. Every audio cue also has a visual one, and haptics on phones.
