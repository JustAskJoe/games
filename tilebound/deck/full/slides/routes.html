ROUTES

Between towns, the tall grass rustles.

Wild tiles

A critter tile pops out of the grass. Catch it by finishing a quick 5-tile puzzle hand in a few draws. Two minutes, no pressure.

Travelers

Hikers, birders and picnickers who want a quick hand. Each one teaches one trick before the town’s master tests it.

Finds

Tea leaves, flowers and trinkets to gift your club friends or use to decorate the clubhouse.

Each route is short: a walk, three travelers and a handful of wild tiles. The point is a change of scenery and a reason to collect, not a grind.
