A growing game meets a loop everyone knows.

From the mahjong table

  • A card of target hands that changes
  • Passing tiles before the hand starts
  • Jokers that fill in a set
  • The click of beautiful tiles
  • Friends around a table every week

From creature-collecting adventures

  • Pick a starter companion
  • Travel town to town by route
  • Beat each town’s master
  • Collect them all, trade the extras
  • A story you can finish

Tilebound: the tiles are critters, each parlor’s card is a quest, and the club is home.

The 1998 handheld trading-card adventure is the structural inspiration: clubs, masters, booster packs, a small world. It’s nostalgia for some players and a fresh loop for others. Everything is original: no names, creatures or icons borrowed.
