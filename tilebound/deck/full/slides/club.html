CLUB LIFE

The Tile & Teacup is home base.

Friendship hearts

Play, chat and bring small gifts. Each heart opens a scene, a story and a tile.

Short, branching chats

Two or three choices, warm writing, no wrong answers. Friends remember what you said.

Requests

“Can you win a Blossom hand in Fernhollow?” Friends send you out into the story.

Mahj Night

A weekly four-seat table. Who sits with you depends on who you’ve grown close to.

Decorate the club

Tablecloths, lamps, plants and racks, earned in the story or bought as cosmetics.

Light romance

Two or three optional storylines after the slice. Sweet, never required.
