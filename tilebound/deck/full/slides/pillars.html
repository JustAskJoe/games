DESIGN PILLARS

Four promises every feature has to keep.

The tiles are treasures

Every tile is a critter worth collecting. Your outfit, your tile set and your table are how you show off to your club.

Your club, your people

Mahjong is social. The club, its friends and weekly Mahj Night are the heart of the game, not a side menu.

Learn by playing

No rulebook up front. Each rule arrives inside a hand that needs it. Experts can skip straight to the full game.

An evening, not a grind

A hand fits a cup of tea. A town fits an evening. The island has an ending.
