Illustrated 2D in a 1920s Art Nouveau mahjong-set style with a light touch of folk magic. Each suit is a critter family whose numbers are growth stages: 1–3 young, 4–6 grown, 7–9 final form. Tiles are built in layers (frame, critter painting, finish), so new tile sets are cheap to make.
