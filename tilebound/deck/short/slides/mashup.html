THE IDEA

A growing game meets a loop everyone knows.

From the mahjong table

  • A card of target hands
  • Passing tiles before each hand
  • Jokers that fill a set
  • Friends around a table every week

From creature-collecting adventures

  • Pick a starter companion
  • Travel town to town
  • Beat each town’s master
  • Collect them all, finish the story

In Tilebound the tiles are critters, each parlor’s card is a quest, and the club is home.
