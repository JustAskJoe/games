HOW IT PLAYS

Real American-style mahjong, four at a table.

1 · The Swap

Pass 3 tiles you don’t want, three times.

2 · Draw, discard

Take a tile, give one back to the pond.

3 · Call

Grab a discard that finishes a set of 3 or more.

4 · Mahj!

Match a hand on the House Card to win.

Never played? A counter shows how many tiles you still need, your closest hands light up, and a hint picks your discard. Club players switch it all off.
