THE CRITTER DECK

A full 52-card deck where every suit is a critter family.

♥ Ember

Hearth critters: moles, kiln-backs, salamanders.

♦ Tide

Pond critters: frogs, koi, manatees.

♣ Grove

Garden critters: mice, badgers, deer.

♠ Dusk

Night critters: owlets, ravens, moth-foxes. Always trump.

2–5 young, 6–10 grown. A card’s rank is the critter’s growth stage, so the deck reads at a glance.

Jack, Queen, King are the court. The Ace is each family’s legend. You collect new art for every card.
