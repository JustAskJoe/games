THE ADVENTURE

An island of card clubs, each with a house rule.

Blind Nil Bar

Bid nil before you look at your cards for 200 points.

Joker Lodge

Big and Little Joker join the deck as top trumps.

Mirror House

Everyone must bid exactly as many spades as they hold.

Suicide Club

One partner on each team must bid nil every hand.

The loop: beat a club’s master team → win their stamp and new critter cards → teach your partner a new duo move → sail to the next town.
