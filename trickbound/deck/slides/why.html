WHY SPADES

Every card is a reaction. Every bid is a promise.

Reactive

Four players, one card each, every trick. You answer what was just played.

A promise

You and your partner bid a number of tricks together, then have to make it.

A partner

Someone across the table to read, cover and celebrate with.

Fast

A hand takes about three minutes on a phone. Perfect for a bus ride.

What Tilebound taught us: in playtest, mahjong felt like drawing alone. Spades gives every turn a decision that answers someone else.
