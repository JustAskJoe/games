YOUR PARTNER

The friend across the table.

Pick your first partner from the club. They have a personality, a bidding style and things to say about your plays.

Bond grows as you win together. Higher bond unlocks duo moves, like a shared nil or a signal you both know.

Table talk built in. Bravado when you set the other team, groans when you go bags.
