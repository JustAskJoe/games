HOW IT PLAYS

Real Spades, four at a table, two teams.

1 · Deal 13

Everyone gets 13 cards. Your partner sits across from you.

2 · Bid

Say how many tricks you will take. Team bids add up. Feeling brave? Bid nil.

3 · Play tricks

Follow the suit that was led. Can’t? Trump with a spade.

4 · Score

Make your bid for 10 a trick. Too many extras, called bags, cost you.

Never played? A bid helper counts your sure tricks, a glowing card shows a good play, and your partner explains why. Seasoned players switch it all off.

Classic Spades scoring: 10 points per trick bid when the team makes it, minus 10 per trick when it fails; each overtrick is a bag worth 1 point, and 10 bags cost 100. Nil is worth 100. First team to 500 wins. These are the Classic table rules; town clubs add house rules on top.
