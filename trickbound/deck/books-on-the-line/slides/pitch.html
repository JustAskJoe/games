THE PITCH

Pokémon TCG on the Game Boy, but the game at the table is Spades.

You ride a dying railway’s last season, card room to card room, with a partner across the table and a deck full of critters that wake up when the game gets real.

Why it can work: Spades is one of the most social card games there is. Everybody plays with a partner. Nobody has turned it into an adventure you can collect your way through.
