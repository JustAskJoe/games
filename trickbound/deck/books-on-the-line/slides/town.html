This is the real prototype, not a mock-up. The opening plays like the first minutes of a Pokémon game: pick a look and a name, wake on the night train, step off at Cinder Hollow, talk to Dee at the depot door, wake two critters, and walk in for a short first duel. After that the town is a hub: gold labels show the back room, the two pickup crews and the train. The pixel art is drawn in code as a placeholder. The final look is the art crew's call; the mood images on the art slide point toward painterly people and cozy isometric rooms. Story layers: the town screen is where the world talks, the train car is where the people talk, and the back room is where the story happens.
