CATCHING · EVERYONE PLAYS

Every crew keeps a critter asleep on the table.

Find a crew. Kids on the platform, the shift crew at the slag heap. Short games to 100.

Play how it likes. A made nil, a set, an exact bid. Its meter fills.

Roll to wake it. Lanterns help. Never 100%, so there’s always one more game.

Plus lamp-lit shinies, temperament like IVs, and natures that change how a critter talks.

This is the Gwent idea: everyone on the Line plays Books, so every group you meet is a possible game. The back room in each depot is the gym that moves the story; pickup crews outside are the side games, and they are where you catch critters. Odds: 15% base plus half the meter, capped at 75%; halved if you lose; lanterns add 10 to 35 points, never above 90%. A missed critter goes back into the town's pool, except a few one-time story critters. Temperament: Nerve, Patience and Spark, each 0 to 15; a sharp-nerve critter gets a slightly stronger ability. Lamp-lit odds are about 1 in 200. Playable now in Cinder Hollow; the other towns come next.
