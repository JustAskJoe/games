These are rendered by the prototype itself, not mock-ups. Packs cost marks at each depot counter: five cards, one uncommon or better, and a bonus slot that can be a lamp-lit foil or, at Copperbank, a misprint. Duplicates sell back for marks. Cards are cosmetic only: you choose which version of each card shows in your hand in the deck builder, and they all play the same. Promos so far: the Going Dark Deuce (make a blind nil), the Full Shift Ace (take all 13 books), the Brakeman's Deuce (win a book with the 2 of spades) and the Ironmere Lantern Ace (the junction badge). Final card art is an art-crew job: courts, Aces and promos get the art budget; pip cards stay simple.
