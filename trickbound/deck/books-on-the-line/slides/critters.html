LANTERN CRITTERS

Your deck is alive, and it talks back.

Miners kept little spirits in their decks for luck. They wake at a real table and bend one rule when it counts.

Each critter is also a voice in your head, Disco Elysium style. They are the coach, and they don’t always agree:

Salamander: “Lead the night. Make them sweat.”

Badger: “That’s enough books. Duck. Bags bite.”

Familiars in the prototype today: Owlet (peek at partner), Salamander (lead a spade early), Koi (change your bid by one), Badger (bags shield), and the grown forms Raven, Kilnback, Frog and Mouse. A 1,500-game bot simulator keeps each one inside a balance band. The voices reuse the helper logic we already have; it is mostly writing. Players pick how chatty: off, quiet or full.
