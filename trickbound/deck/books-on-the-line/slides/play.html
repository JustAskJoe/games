HOW IT PLAYS · BOOKS

Real Spades underneath, with a world on top.

1 · Bid

13 cards each. Say how many books (tricks) you and your partner will take.

2 · Play

Follow suit if you can. Spades are “night” and beat everything.

3 · Bend

Your lantern critter breaks one rule when it counts. Everyone sees it.

4 · Win the night

Make your bid, beat the house team, ride on to the next town.

Every depot keeps its own house rule: the miner’s count (bid your spade count), going dark (blind nil), the long night (a nil every hand), Conductor & Brakeman (two jokers).

Rules are standard Spades: 10 points per book bid, overtricks are bags, 10 bags costs 100, nil is plus or minus 100, blind nil plus or minus 200. The house rules are real Spades variants given folk names. A Classic table ("the old way") is always available with no critters and no helpers.
