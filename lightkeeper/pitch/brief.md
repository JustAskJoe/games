# Lightkeeper · Brief

Solo pitch from Zach, answered in Forge and approved there on November 1, 2026. Hunter and Joe haven't answered or discussed it yet, so treat every decision below as a draft until the crew talks it through.

## The hook
A one thumb night sea puzzle. You keep a lighthouse. Sweep the beam across the water: every ship in your light turns toward the harbor, and every ship in the dark holds its course. You never touch a ship. You bring a whole fleet home past the reefs by choosing who to light, and when.

## Signature mechanic: Light means "come to me"
The beam does exactly one thing. Lit ships bend toward the lamp; dark ships sail straight. Because every lit ship turns toward the same point, routing around a reef is about timing, not aim. Light a ship early and it beelines home through the rocks. Hold it dark until it clears the reef, then light it.

## Decisions
- **Player:** casual mobile players who like calm, thinky puzzles. Easy to start, hard to master.
- **Platform:** phone first web kit for the prototype; iOS, Android and Steam later if it holds up.
- **Modes:** solo campaign of nights; daily night with a fixed seed as a stretch.
- **Controls:** drag anywhere to aim the beam. Tap is the foghorn: ships near the tap slow down for a moment, for spacing them out. No precision taps on boats.
- **Nights:** 60 to 120 seconds, sessions of 5 to 15 minutes. Three wrecks ends the night; retry is one tap.
- **Loop:** read the sea, sweep the beam, dock the fleet, next night.
- **Progression:** a map of 10 to 15 nights for a first release. Up to 3 stars per night (all in, no wrecks, par time). New lenses (wide, split, pulse) unlock at set nights as new tools, never as stat upgrades.
- **World:** a lighthouse on a rocky point by a fishing harbor, before radio. Short keeper's log lines between nights set up each twist. Ships have names, shown when they dock.
- **Art:** flat vector with a linocut print feel. Ink blue night, carved cream lines, one warm amber beam. Only the beam, ship lanterns and the harbor mouth are bright. Dark ships show a dotted heading line.
- **Audio:** sea wash, a slow minor drone, a bell per docked ship that climbs the scale, a wooden knock for a wreck, a foghorn. A perfect night resolves into a chord. All synthesized in code.
- **Business:** try free, buy once. Sell cosmetics and night packs. Never hints or extra wrecks for money.

## Prototype 1
6 nights, 1 lens, 1 ship type, reefs, a sideways current, fog, a storm, and the foghorn tap. Built in the phone first web kit with a deterministic engine and a bot sim.

**The one question it answers:** is steering ships only by lighting them, never touching them, a puzzle people want to solve again?
**Pass bar:** players retry a failed night without being asked, and finish all 6.

## Risks
- The beam may feel too indirect at first. The first three nights must teach it fast.
- Calm puzzles are crowded; the one verb hook has to read in a 5 second clip.
- Keep to one ship type until the core is proven.
