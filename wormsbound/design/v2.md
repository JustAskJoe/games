# Wormsbound v2: bigger fights on fresh maps

© 2026 justaskjoe. All rights reserved.

Written Nov 11, 2026 by Hunter (with Claude). A proposal for Louiemark, who leads Wormsbound. It builds on the map generator ([`mapgen.md`](mapgen.md)) and comes before any "champion / hero" work: the goal is a solid base game first.

## What v2 is
Four things Hunter asked for, built together because each needs the others:

1. **A fresh map every match, in every mode.** Not a pick from a list: each round rolls new settings from a recipe for the mode being played.
2. **More units on the map:** crews of 2 to 5 a side, up to 12 units in a match.
3. **Bigger maps** that grow with the number of units, with a camera.
4. **Spawns that are planned for each map** instead of fixed slots.

Added the same day at Hunter's request: **five more characters** (ten in five classes), **a gear slot** with a first 14 items from the 100-weapon list, and **pixel sprites** for every character (sections 8 to 10). Still not in v2: crates, levelling, pick and ban, or a deeper hero mechanic (see [Next](#next-after-v2)).

## Decisions (Hunter, Nov 11)
| Question | Answer |

## 1. Fresh maps: recipes
`MapGen.RECIPES` (in `prototype/mapgen.js`). A recipe is a pool of themes (all ten, for now) and a low-to-high range for any setting; anything it doesn't list uses the theme's own default. `MapGen.roll(recipe, seed)` picks from it with the round's seed, so the server, every browser and a replay all roll the same map without sending it.

| Recipe | Used by | Leans towards | Mirrored | Spawns | Start under cover |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `duel` | 1v1 (2 Players, online 1v1) | moderate everything, few hazards | 20% of rounds | each side on its half | never |
| `solo` | 1 Player | gentler ground and wind, few hazards, no hard rock | never | dealt out in turn | never |
| `ffa` | online free-for-all | broken ground, more caves and platforms, hazards on | never | dealt out in turn | 15% of units |
| `squads` | Squads | high ground, tunnels, overhangs, some hard rock | 15% | together or mixed, by seed | 25% |
| `teams` | online team battle | wide solid battlefield, moderate ruggedness | 30% | each team together | 20% |

The title screen's map list now starts with **Fresh map every round** (the default). Shuffle (the ten theme maps with their usual settings), a preset, a Custom Map and a share code all still work and override the recipe.

`G.map` holds the rolled map's share code, so a replay or a bug report can rebuild it.

## 2. Map size
`MapGen.sizeFor(units)`: the engine asks for it unless `opts.mapSize` says otherwise.

| Units in the match | Size | Grid | Screens wide |
|---|---|---|---|
| up to 4 | Medium | 160 × 90 | 1 (no camera) |
| 5 or 6 | Large | 200 × 112 | 1.25 |
| 7 or 8 | Huge | 240 × 135 | 1.5 |
| 9 to 12 | Giant (new) | 320 × 180 | 2 |

The engine no longer assumes 160 × 90: every match has its own `G.cols` and `G.rows`. A full-power shot still travels about 120 cells, so on a Giant map you can't hit the far end from your own: you fight who is near, and walk.

## 3. Crews
- `opts.size` is 1 to 5 units a side (`MAX_SIZE`), with at most 12 units in a match (`MAX_UNITS`).
- **Every unit has its own kit:** its character, its Shot 2 and dud ammo, and its own SS charge (one notch per turn of its own). In v1 these belonged to the side. With one unit a side nothing changes.
- `opts.chars` takes one character per side (the whole crew plays it) or one per unit.
- `opts.owners` says who controls each unit. Squads don't use it. Team battle gives every unit its own player, or -1 for a computer.
- **Health by crew size:** 2 a side ×1.5, 3 ×1.25, 4 ×1.05, 5 ×0.9 (`CREW_HP`). 1v1 and free-for-all keep their v1 numbers.
- **Sudden death waits longer for bigger crews:** a "round" is one turn per side, and crews get ×1.35 (2 a side) to ×2.4 (5 a side) as many turns before the water rises. The rise scales with the map's height.

## 4. Spawns
`newGame` plans them for the map it just built:
- **Spots** are spread across the width, each on dry ground at least 5 cells from a channel, and 5 to 8 cells from anyone else (closer when there are more units).
- **Layout:** `sides` keeps each side together (side 0 on the left in a two-sided match); `mixed` deals the spots out one side at a time, so crews start in among each other. The recipe chooses; mirrored maps always use `sides` and mirror the spots.
- **Under cover:** a share of units (the recipe's `under`) start on a ledge or cave floor below the surface instead of on top, with 5 cells of headroom.
- Hazards within 7 cells of a spawn are still removed.

Not built: units arriving mid-match (reinforcements, respawns). See open question 3.

## 5. Camera
Only on maps bigger than one screen; a 160 × 90 match looks and plays exactly as before.
- **Follows** the unit whose turn it is, then the shot, then anyone falling.
- **Look around:** right-drag (or two fingers, or left-drag when it isn't your turn). **Zoom:** wheel, pinch, or − and =. **M** shows the whole map; **C** (or Follow) goes back.
- **Minimap** top left: ground, liquid, every unit, the shot, and the part you're looking at. Click it to jump there.
- **Arrows** on the screen's edge point to living units you can't see.
- Aiming is unchanged: drag back anywhere and let go.

## 6. Modes
| Mode | Sides × units | Map | New in v2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Player | 4 × 1, three computers | Medium, `solo` recipe | fresh maps |
| 2 Players | 2 × 1, one keyboard | Medium, `duel` | fresh maps |
| **Squads** (new) | 2 × 2 to 5, against Player 2 or an Easy / Medium / Hard computer | Medium to Giant, `squads` | all of it. Pick each crew one unit at a time, or "Fill the rest for me". |
| Online 1v1 | 2 × 1 | Medium, `duel` | fresh maps |
| Online free-for-all | 2 to 6 × 1 | Medium or Large, `ffa` | fresh maps, Large map and camera at 5 or 6 players |
| **Online team battle** (new) | 2 × 2 to 5, one player per unit | Medium to Giant, `teams` | all of it |

### Online team battle
- A room has two teams of up to 5 slots (seats 0 to 4 and 5 to 9). Joining puts you on the team with fewer players; you can switch in the lobby. The host sets units a side (2 to 5) and presses Start. One round a match, then back to the room.
- **Every player plays one unit.** A slot nobody sits in is played by a computer on the server (Medium), with a character picked from the seed. One person can start a 5v5 alone, with nine computers.
- **A player who drops** has their unit played by a computer until they come back.
- The server paces its computers: each waits until the last shot has landed on everyone's screen, plus a moment.
- The protocol gained `team`, `size`, and `mode: 'teams'`; the room message gained `size`. Details at the top of `server/room.js`.

## 7. Computers on big maps
Bots used to stand still. On a map wider than one screen, a bot with nobody in reach now walks towards the nearest enemy first, and never steps into the liquid or off a long drop (`safeStep`). On one-screen maps they behave as before.

## 8. The cast: ten characters, five classes
Added Nov 11 (Hunter: "a cast of 10 characters for this v2, balanced, with abilities"). Five new characters, one more for each of five **classes**. All are built from shot fields the engine already had, plus one new one: a negative `knock` **pulls** units towards the blast.

| Class | Original | New (v2) | What the new one does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tank | Brick | **Tusk** | Less tough than Brick, but every shot shoves. Bouncer bounces twice; Stampede is three ram shells. |
| Scout | Zip | **Hopper** | Climbs the highest ledges of anyone (4 cells). Pebble is the cheapest turn in the game; Skip Shot skips twice; Flurry is seven pebbles. |
| Gunner | Volt | **Flint** | Fills the air: Scatter Flares (four in a wide fan), Barrage (five in a tight one). |
| Engineer | Digger | **Patch** | Short digs with wide blasts (Shaped Charge), and Sinkhole: a huge crater plus a tremor. |
| Controller | Gale | **Nimbus** | Moves people. Vacuum pulls everyone towards the blast; Thunderhead is three close lightning bolts. |

**Balance (bot sim, smart vs smart, 100 matches a pair, 45 pairs):** every character wins 44 to 56% of its matches (the band is 40 to 60%). Numbers are first guesses tuned in three passes; nobody has played the new five.

## 9. Gear: a loadout slot from the 100-weapon list
Hunter asked whether the loadout and the 100 weapons ([`weapons.md`](weapons.md)) should go in now, and how to balance them. What's built is the **system and a first 14 items**, not all 100:

- **Every unit carries one piece of gear**, chosen before the match, in a 4th shot slot (key 4). It has its own ammo (1 to 3 a match) and its own turn delay.
- **Classes limit it.** Four items anyone can carry; two more for each class. So every character chooses from six, and a Tank can never carry a Controller's Gravity Well.
- Every character has a usual pick, so nobody has to choose (Enter takes it). Computers take the usual one half the time.
- The 14 come from the weapons list: mostly ones marked R there ("reuses what the engine has"), plus four that needed a little new code: bomblets (`cluster`), adding ground (`fill`), turn delay on a hit (`slow`) and the pull.

| Gear | # in the list | Who | A match | What |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pocket Pistol | 6 | anyone | 3 | Two quick weak shots; the cheapest turn |
| Rivet Rifle | 1 | anyone | 2 | Three fast flat shots the wind hardly touches |
| Sky Lobber | 7 | anyone | 2 | A slow, heavy lob |
| Popcorn Bomb | 35 | anyone | 1 | Pops into five bomblets |
| Crater Maker | 72 | Tank | 1 | One enormous hole |
| Ricochet Repeater | 4 | Tank | 2 | Up to three bounces |
| Scattercan | 2 | Scout | 2 | Six pellets in a wide fan |
| Triple Barrel | 14 | Scout | 2 | Three shells, five degrees apart |
| TNT Drop | 37 | Gunner | 1 | Three heavy bombs |
| Pinball Shot | 9 | Gunner | 2 | Up to four bounces |
| Thermite Spike | 48 | Engineer | 2 | Burns through 20 cells, then explodes |
| Dirt Cannon | 53 | Engineer | 2 | Adds ground: build a wall, plug a tunnel |
| Gravity Well | 51 | Controller | 2 | Pulls everyone in, then pops |
| Delay Ray | 65 | Controller | 2 | Enemies it catches take their next turn later (+300 delay) |

### How it's balanced (and how more gets added)
1. **Gear is a sidegrade, not an upgrade.** The sim plays a character carrying each item against the same character with none. The band is 38 to 64% (200 matches is about ±7%); all 14 sit between 43 and 57%.
2. **Three knobs per item:** ammo, turn delay, and the blast itself. A strong item is rare and slow (Crater Maker: 1 a match, the biggest delay in the game).
3. **Classes stop the worst combinations** before they need numbers: the pull stays with the two characters who are already about moving people.
4. **The character band still has to hold with usual gear on**, so a character can't be fixed by its gear.
5. **Adding an item** is one entry in `GEAR` (`engine.js`), a number from `weapons.md`, and a sim line that appears on its own. If it needs new behaviour, it also needs a line in the bots' `scoreLanding`, or the sim can't judge it.

What this is not yet: the weapons list's **crates, the per-match deck and in-match levelling**. Those are a second way to get the same items (find them mid-match instead of bringing them), and they can sit on top of `GEAR` unchanged.

Limits: the bots never fire the Dirt Cannon (they don't build), so its sim line only shows it does no harm. Online rooms use each character's usual gear; there's no gear picker in the lobby yet.

## 10. Unit sprites, drawn in code
Hunter asked for the art to be made the way Joe made Rogue Shooter's: pixel art drawn by scripts, no image model. `art/tools/units.py` (Pillow) draws all ten characters on a 32 × 32 grid with three frames each (two idle, one hurt), light from the top-left and a dark outline, and writes `art/sprites/units.png`. `build.py` inlines the sheet; the game swaps three key colours for each team's, so one sheet serves all six teams. A contact sheet for people is in `art/concepts/units.png`. If the sheet is missing, the game falls back to the old code-drawn bodies.

This is a first pass for the crew to react to, not final art. Terrain, backgrounds, shots and UI are unchanged. The lead art plan (its own pull request) still decides the direction.

## Numbers (bot sim, smart vs smart)
From the `crew` lines of `node sim.js 200` and a 24-match check on Nov 11 (20 to 24 matches a format, so rough):

| Format | Map | Median turns | Slowest 10% | Reach sudden death | Timeouts | Stuck |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2v2 | 160 × 90 | 23 | 35+ | about 1 in 3 | 0 | 0 |
| 3v3 | 200 × 112 | 26 | 47+ | about 1 in 3 | 0 | 0 |
| 5v5 squads | 320 × 180 | 63 | 74+ | over half | 0 | 0 |
| 5v5 team battle | 320 × 180 | 64 | 77+ | over half | 0 | 0 |
| 6-way free-for-all | 200 × 112 | 46 | 57+ | 1 in 20 | 0 | 0 |
| 4 crews of 3 | 320 × 180 | 89 | 100+ | 2 in 3 | 0 | 0 |

- Side 0 won 45 to 50% of the two-sided formats, so neither spawn layout favours a side.
- A first pass had a 5v5 at a median of 78 turns. Crews of 5 now have 10% less health each and the water rises sooner (58 turns in, was 72), which brought it to 63. That is still about six turns per unit: see open question 1.
- The v1 balance bands (1v1 mirror, skill, duds, the five characters, 1 Player difficulty) all still pass on fresh maps.

## Open questions for Louiemark
1. **5v5 match length.** A median of 63 turns is long. Options: less health for crews of 5, sudden death sooner, or faster turns (15 seconds) in big matches. Which feels right when people play it?
2. **SS charge in crews.** Each unit charges its own SS on its own turns, so a 5v5 sees its first SS around turn 30. Should a crew share one SS meter instead?
3. **Units arriving mid-match.** "Dynamic spawning" could also mean reinforcements: a knocked-out unit's crewmate parachuting in, or a late joiner dropping into a team battle. Not built. Worth it?
4. **Squads against more than one computer crew** (three crews of 3, four of 3). The engine and sim handle it; the menu only offers two sides.
5. **Team battle with fewer bots.** Today empty slots are always computers. Should the host be able to close them (a 2v1)?
6. **Online host picks the map.** Online rounds always roll from the recipe. A host-chosen preset or share code needs one more room message.
7. **The new five** (Tusk, Hopper, Flint, Patch, Nimbus): names, roles and shots are first guesses. Keep, rename, rework?
8. **Gear:** is one slot with class limits the right first step towards the weapons list, and which items next? Should crates come back as a second way to get gear?
9. **Recipes:** are the five the right ones, and should a recipe ever fix the theme (a "Frostbite night" playlist)?
